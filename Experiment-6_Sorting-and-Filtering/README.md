# Experiment 6: Sorting and Filtering (Sales Performance Analysis)

**Files:** `BA_EXP06.pdf` (report), `Sales dataset_Sorting and Filtering.pbix` (Power BI file)

## Business question
How can sorting and filtering help a sales manager find high-value transactions, compare regions and categories, and spot underperforming or loss-making products?

## Data
Excel sales dataset with 300 transactions (October 2025 to September 2026), 12 products and four regions. Fields: Transaction ID, Date, Region, Product, Category, Quantity, Revenue, Profit, Discount, Returned.

## What was done
- Imported the Excel data and formatted revenue and profit as currency, discount and margin as percentages.
- Created a calendar table for chronological monthly analysis.
- Wrote DAX measures: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Transactions, Profit Margin % and loss-making transactions.
- Sorted transactions and products by revenue and profit, and ordered monthly revenue chronologically.
- Filtered with slicers and visual-level filters by date, region, category and product.

## Tools
Power BI Desktop, Power Query, DAX
