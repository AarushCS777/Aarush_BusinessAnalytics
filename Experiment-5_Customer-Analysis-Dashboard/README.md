# Experiment 5: Customer Analysis Dashboard

**Files:** `BA_Exp5_Report.pdf` (report with dashboard screenshots), `BA_Exp5.pbix` (Power BI file)

## Business questions
- Which product category has the most purchasing activity?
- Which payment method is used most?
- How does revenue change month to month?
- Which customers purchase most often?
- Does a small group of customers contribute a large share of revenue?

## Data
Customer and order CSV files (order table named `customer_analysis_orders_5000` in the report), related through CustomerID.

## DAX measures
Total Revenue, Total Orders, Purchasing Customers, Average Order Value and Customer Purchase Count (formulas are listed in the PDF).

## Findings reported in the PDF
- By order count, Electronics is the most popular category, followed closely by Fashion.
- UPI is the most frequently used payment method (about 33% of orders), ahead of Credit Card and Debit Card.

See the PDF for the monthly revenue analysis and the customer concentration analysis.

## Tools
Power BI Desktop, DAX
