# Superstore Sales and Profit Dashboard

**Files:** `Superstore_sales&profit.pdf` (report), `Superstore_sales&profit.pbix` (Power BI file)

## Business question
How do sales and profit differ across product categories, sub-categories, regions and products?

## Data
Sample Superstore dataset (from Kaggle). Main columns used: Sales, Profit, Quantity, Order ID, Category, Sub-Category, Region, Product Name.

## What was done
- Checked and set data types in Power Query (Order Date and Ship Date as dates; Sales, Quantity and Profit as numeric).
- Built KPI cards for Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity and Total Orders.
- Built charts comparing sales and profit by category, region and sub-category, plus a Top 10 Products chart.
- Added slicers for interactive filtering.

## Tools
Power BI Desktop, Power Query
